# Scorecard — scoped adventure/equipment presentation

1. **Good design is innovative — 2/3**
   Evidence: E3. The objective dock improves an established idle/RPG loop; no claim of a unique pattern across peer products is justified.
2. **Good design makes a product useful — 2/3**
   Evidence: E3. Core tasks complete, while preparation and recipe pages still add navigation steps.
3. **Good design is aesthetic — 1/3**
   Evidence: E1. Painted heroes against pixel enemies/landscapes are one jarring visible violation of a single style.
4. **Good design makes a product understandable — 1/3**
   Evidence: E2/E4. Locked map controls are unnamed, next destination is absent, and order/retreat terminology adds ambiguity.
5. **Good design is unobtrusive — 3/3**
   Evidence: E3. Workshop art is the figure and restrained unboxed controls recede; full-screen pages isolate information.
6. **Good design is honest — 1/3**
   Evidence: E4. Multiple labels overstate or misdescribe consequences, especially final/repeat guardian unlocking and weapon-versus-armor effects; no dark pattern was found.
7. **Good design is long-lasting — 3/3**
   Evidence: E1/E5. Ink/gouache, serif/sans typography and brass/teal palette have no identified trend-specific marker; mixed media is scored under aesthetics.
8. **Good design is thorough down to the last detail — 2/3**
   Evidence: E5. Empty/blocked/success/disabled are considered; distinct keyboard focus is one rough state. Synchronous local pages do not need network loaders; cold start remains unmeasured.
9. **Good design is environmentally friendly — 2/3**
   Evidence: E6. Native adaptation: no web JS/network payload, motion gating present, but texture memory grows and hidden workshop work continues. The web-specific byte thresholds are not represented as native measurements.
10. **Good design is as little design as possible — 2/3**
    Evidence: E3 and structural unused-object section. Most controls have a task; the hidden JourneyDetail and redundant trailing preparation link are removable without changing primary tasks.

**Total:19/30.** Equal integer weights, lower score where evidence is uncertain. Scoring is for current shipped presentation, not proposed generated assets. Evidence anchors are in01-evidence.md.
